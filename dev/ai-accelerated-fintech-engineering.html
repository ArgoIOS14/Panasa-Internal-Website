    The Problem

    Fintech teams are stuck maintaining instead of building

    Most engineering organisations we work with are spending 60-70% of their time on maintenance and compliance remediation.

    What works at a million transactions falls apart at ten million. Exception handling, edge cases, and reconciliation gaps multiply faster than teams can patch them.

    PCI recertification, scheme mandate updates, FCA reporting changes — each one adds weeks to your release cycle when bolted on at the end instead of built in from the start.

    Legacy code written three years ago now consumes most of your sprint capacity. New features get squeezed into whatever time remains, and quality suffers.

    How We Work

    Five stages, continuous feedback

    No need to hire, train, and manage multiple teams. We're your end-to-end fintech engineering and operations partner.

    1. 1 Scope

      Problem framing, acceptance criteria, scheme rules mapped to requirements.

    2. 2 Prototype

      Competing approaches built on separate branches. Working code in days, not weeks.

    3. 3 Validate

      Automated linting, security scans, type checks. Issues caught and fixed before review.

    4. 4 Test

      AI-generated test suites from acceptance criteria. 80%+ coverage enforced at the pipeline level.

    5. 5 Deploy

      Progressive rollout with canary releases, automated rollback triggers, and full observability.
